Lady america, I've been fascinated by you for a long time. The first thing was the videos. The minecraft mod reviews, letsplays, guys screaming into the camera, people making videos about them just playing a videogame that would make me cry. That's how I learned most of the language, actually. At one point I was watching so much of jacksepticeye that I actually started speaking with a noticeable irish accent. The videos were incredible, nothing like it existed on russian youtube in 2015. Then it was reddit, when I was around 13. I got really into the memes. Most russian memes are like, a silly picture and then a swear word in IMPACT font on the bottom. But on reddit there were inside jokes, nuance, irony, post-irony, and so much more. Reddit got me into the culture as well. The fascinating problems you people have. I became very leftist. If a 14 year old boy in russia could vote in US elections, I would have voted for bernie sanders. None of my friends in real life had any context for my references. I watched video essays in english, i read books. I *loved* To Kill A Mockingbird. The book is pure americana. American houses, american people, american problems, american flowers, american families - i gulped it up. I started disliking my birth country at that point too. Russia is full of depressed, angry, boring people. America contains everything interesting, and russia doesn't. Then america turned me trans. Don't get me wrong, I appreciate it a lot. I competed in an high schoolers' english olimpiad, and the government gave me $1000 for almost making finals. A trans boy placed two points above me and did make it to finals. It's only fair - I never really studied english, I just got some basics from tutoring and then absorbed the rest of the language through youtube. I was maybe one of the top 20 high schoolers in russia? I guess if you don't count kids that are living in russia but their parents are native english speakers. God, it's a wonderful language. There's maybe like 7 rules that a 9 year old can understand, and then you just hear new words and remember them and then you speak the language. You can make a noun into an adjective, an adjective into a verb, a verb into a noun, or anything else you please. The sentences are short and to the point, the words are streamlined, the idioms are beautiful and people actually use them. I noticed that my thoughts were in english now. Okay maybe I was a little obsessed with the language. I spoke it better than my teacher. While walking the family dog, I'd monologue out loud to myself in english, talking about things on my mind. Not like anyone is gonna know what I'm saying. It helped with pronouncination I think, but that wasn't really why I did it. A funny quirk of learning english entirely by listening to youtubers talking to a silent audience is that my inner monologue is like that now. I'm always talking to an audience in my head, so it feels natural to monologue out loud. I really dislike little imperfections in my english, so I got really good at not making mistakes, just by googling things. 

I wonder what it's like inside you, America. Are there fire hydrants everywhere? Well-kept lawns? Probably a lot of fat people, and not a lot of children. Double-story family houses, with that short little fense that separates your house where you sleep from the chaos of the outside. There's people that carry guns with them. That's strange. Your cities are small, but there's a LOT of them, quite close together. It would be so cool to go on a road trip. Only americans do road trips. In a van with friends? That would be cool. Black people everywhere. You know, there's not a lot of black people in russia or kazakhstan (where i'm living now). There used to be two black kids at my 3000 person school - a brother and a sister. I talked to the girl once, she liked steven universe, so in a funny way i felt like i had more in common with her than i had with most other kids at school. Also sometimes if you rode the moscow underground you'd sometimes see black people, they were always very very well-dressed for some reason. 

I'm probably an [ameriboo](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Ameriboo). I sometimes worry that it won't live up to my expectations. What if the cities are too small and too same-ey? What if I get tired of being around crazy americans all day? What if transphobia is actually worse in america compared to kazakhstan? I have other worries too. Of course someday I want to move to america, but what if my papers are wrong? What if it takes such a long time that we all die before i get there? What if trump makes it hard for russian citizens, or trans girls, to go? I hate myself because i want to go, i really do, but i'm scared and i never feel like i'm earning enough money to start doing it, and i'm scared that maybe i'll go but then i'll have to leave and that would break my heart. I kind of dread my first month in america, actually. There's so many people to talk to and so many places to visit, i would be so overwhelmed. 

Then at 18 i moved to kazakhstan. Learning english has been the most important thing i've ever done for my life, but getting on twitter was also pretty great. The first girl i really started talking to became my first ever girlfriend, but it was a long-distance relationship. She introduced me to some of her friends, and i started reading lesswrong, and my part of twitter just kind of pulled me in and now when I post a random thought, at least 500 people see it, and if i post a *good* thought, it's tens of thousands. I met some people from twitter in real life, and without fail they were just incredible amazing shinybest people. Each one of them was living their life, in a way i'd never seen a person do. Most people around me just spend their days. I do to, I guess, and that's scary. I hope I'm good enough to be among the people I admire, and I'm hope I'm good enough soon enough that I get to live in america. If I'm having sex with a person these days, it's a major turn-off if they don't speak english. I guess these days there's people translating stuff into russian, a schoolmate of mine recently showed me a channel that makes video-essays about rationalist cultural exports, think roko's basillisk, technosingularity, etc. I showed him that in my notifications on twitter eliezer yudkowsky has liked a tweet of mine. I think his enthusiasm was fake.
